Feeling lazy? No problem

Recording an expense takes seconds

  • Say it — and it’s recorded

    Tap the mic and say “taxi yesterday 2400 kaspi” — amount, category, date and account are picked up on their own. Or type it in one line.

  • Receipt to categories in a second

    Point the camera at the receipt QR code — purchases sort into categories on their own: groceries here, household goods there.

  • Statements instead of typing

    Upload a bank PDF statement — transactions add themselves, and nothing you already entered gets doubled. Kaspi and Halyk now, more banks on the way.

  • An evening nudge

    If nothing was recorded that day, it gently reminds you in the evening. Nothing else.

Not AI — real financial math

An assistant that does the math for you

The elephant goes through every expense and tells you what to do next: set money aside, save up, pay off a debt.

Under the hood is not a neural network that might make up a number, but a calculation model from financial planning practice: essential costs, savings rate, debt load, the “avalanche” method for early repayment. Every figure is computed from your data, and you can see where it came from. Same data — always the same answer.

  • How much you can spend until payday — after essential costs, loan payments and money set aside for goals
  • Where money leaks — spots small frequent expenses — coffee, delivery, taxis — that add up to a fortune over a year
  • Safety cushion — works out how much you need based on your essential costs and tracks how much you have
  • Which loan to pay off first — shows where early repayment pays off most and how much interest you’ll save
  • Monthly pace — warns you early if you’re spending faster than planned and shows a daily allowance
  • A step-by-step plan — see your money → start saving → protect yourself → get debt under control

Everything is calculated on your phone — your data never leaves it. These are tips for your own decisions, not financial advice.

Your data is yours

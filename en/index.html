Feeling lazy? No problem

Recording an expense takes seconds

  • Say it — and it’s recorded

    Tap the mic and say “taxi yesterday 2400 kaspi” — amount, category, date and account are picked up on their own. Or type it in one line.

  • Receipt to categories in a second

    Point the camera at the receipt QR code — purchases sort into categories on their own: groceries here, household goods there.

  • Statements instead of typing

    Upload a bank PDF statement — transactions add themselves, and nothing you already entered gets doubled. Kaspi and Halyk now, more banks on the way.

  • The elephant has tips

    How much you can spend until payday and where money leaks — no spreadsheets, no formulas.

Love being in control? It’s all here

Tools a CFO would use, made for a family

  • Category budget — a monthly plan and pace: are you within your limits
  • Cash flow budget — money in, money out and the month-end balance
  • Honest loans — interest separate from principal, bank reconciliation, early repayment
  • Net worth — assets minus debts, revaluation of property and currencies
  • Projects and payees — what the renovation or the trip really cost
  • Goals and envelopes — money set aside right on the account, no extra transfers
  • Multi-currency cards — tenge, dollars and euros on one card, National Bank rates
  • Advisor — a step-by-step plan: safety net, debts, savings

Your data is yours

No sign-up, no servers of ours, no ads. The database lives on your phone, backups can be password-encrypted, and the app unlocks with Face ID. Dark theme included.

Why an elephant

Elephants never forget. Neither does Elefund: every expense in its place, and at the end of the month your balance matches the bank. Made in Almaty by a developer who has spent almost 20 years building financial systems — first for himself.

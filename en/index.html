Every tenge accounted for

Elefund is a personal finance app for iPhone. QR receipts, Kaspi and Halyk statements, budgets and goals. Everything stays on your phone.

for iPhoneComing to the App Store

The basics are free. Pro is a subscription or a one-time purchase.

Receipt to categories in a second

Scan the QR code on a receipt — purchases sort into categories on their own: groceries here, household goods there.

Statements instead of typing

Upload a Kaspi Gold or Halyk PDF statement — transactions add themselves, and nothing you already entered gets doubled.

Your data is yours

No sign-up, no servers of ours, no ads. The database lives on your phone, and backups can be password-encrypted.

Also

  • Quick entry in a single line
  • Category budgets and monthly cash flow
  • Goals and envelopes for savings
  • Loans and debts
  • Advisor: a step-by-step plan — safety net, debts, savings
  • Multiple currencies with National Bank rates
  • Dark theme, Face ID, English, Russian and Kazakh

Why an elephant

Elephants never forget. Neither does Elefund: every expense in its place, and at the end of the month your balance matches the bank. Made in Almaty by someone who has spent almost 20 years building financial systems — first for himself.

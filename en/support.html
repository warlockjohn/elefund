Support

Answers to common questions. Didn’t find yours? Write to us.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your iPhone, inside the app. There is no sign-up and no developer cloud — which is why backups matter.

How do I move my data to a new phone?
  1. On the old phone: More → Backup → Save copy and send the file to yourself (AirDrop, Files, a messenger).
  2. On the new one: install Elefund, open More → Backup → Restore from file and pick the backup.
I forgot my backup password

Unfortunately, an encrypted backup cannot be opened without its password — not by you, not by us. That is how encryption works. If the app is still on your phone, your data is intact: remove or change the password in Backup and save a new backup.

Which statements can I upload?

Currently Kaspi Gold and Halyk Bank PDF statements (including foreign-currency ones). We add other banks as we get samples — tell us which one you need.

Which QR receipts work?

Kazakhstan fiscal receipts with a QR code that can be checked on the fiscal data operator’s website. If a receipt doesn’t work, send us the link from its QR code.

What’s in Pro?

Statement uploads, QR receipts, category budgets and cash flow, the advisor, projects and counterparties, unlimited goals and envelopes, password-protected backups. Accounts, transactions, the journal, reports, loans and backups are free forever.

How do I restore a purchase?

More → Elefund Pro → Restore purchases. Use the same Apple ID you bought with.

How do I cancel my subscription?

iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Elefund → Cancel Subscription. Pro keeps working until the end of the paid period, and all your data stays in place.

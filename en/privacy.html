Privacy Policy

Updated: November 13, 2026

In short: Elefund does not collect your data. No sign-up, no servers of ours, no ads, no analytics. Everything you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

Accounts, transactions, categories, budgets, goals, receipts and settings are stored in a database inside the app on your iPhone. The developer has no access to them.

When the app goes online

  • Exchange rates. The app downloads official rates from the National Bank of Kazakhstan (nationalbank.kz). None of your data is sent.
  • QR receipts. When you scan a receipt, the details from its QR code (cash register number, receipt number, date, amount) are sent to the fiscal data operator that issued it, to get the list of purchases. Nothing else is sent.
  • PDF statements. Statements are processed right on the phone and never sent anywhere.
  • Purchases. Pro payments are processed by Apple. Subscription status is checked with RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous purchase ID and purchase details from Apple — not your financial data, name or email.

Backups

Backups are created on request and automatically, in the app folder on your phone. You can save a backup file anywhere via the Share menu. If you set a password, the backup is encrypted (AES-256) and the password is kept in the iOS Keychain. An encrypted backup cannot be opened without the password — including by the developer.

Permissions

  • Camera — only to scan receipt QR codes. Images are not saved.
  • Face ID — to lock the app. iOS performs the check; the app never receives biometric data.
  • Notifications — for reminders you turn on. They are scheduled on the phone.
  • Files and photos — only the ones you pick: a statement, a backup, a screenshot for a message to the developer.

Messages to the developer

“Contact the developer” opens the standard Share menu: you choose where and what to send. A technical line is added to your text — app version, iOS version, phone model and language — to make problems easier to solve.

Deleting data

Delete the app and all its data is removed from the phone. You can also erase data in the app settings. Backups you saved yourself stay wherever you saved them.

Children

The app is not intended for children under 13 and does not collect data about them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
